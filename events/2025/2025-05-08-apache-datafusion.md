In this talk, Mustafa Akur will take you behind the scenes of query execution
with Apache DataFusion. You'll explore how queries are parsed, optimized, and
executed, and gain insight into the architectural principles that make
DataFusion fast, extensible, and modular. The talk will delve into the goals of
the Apache DataFusion project, the problems it aims to solve in the modern data
ecosystem, and why Rust is a key part in the story.
