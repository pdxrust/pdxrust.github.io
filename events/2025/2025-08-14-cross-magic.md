Being inspired by magic word squares after last month's talk, Ben Massey decided
to craft a demo of an AI-powered crossword game using these word squares.
Touching on approaches to personal projects, the state of generative AI usage in
Rust, and front-end crate options: Ben is aiming to use this demo game to bring
up some fun Rust topics.

Ben is a software developer at Portland design firm Downstream, who has made
Rust his primary language for personal projects, and has always had an interest
in interactive experiences.
