Two years after his first TetaNES talk, Luke Petherbridge returns with new and
exciting features for his NES emulator: a full CPU debugger and a libretro core
that runs inside RetroArch.

The debugger has breakpoints, watch expressions, a disassembler, a PPU viewer
and memory editing. Luke built most of it with the assistance of an LLM coding
agent. He'll show what worked, what didn't, and how he kept hundreds of
generated commits reviewable.
